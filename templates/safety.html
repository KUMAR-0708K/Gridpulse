PUBLIC SAFETY DIRECTIVE

Electrical Hazard Guidelines & Emergency Protocol

High voltage overhead lines carry between 11,000 Volts (11kV) and 66,000 Volts. In case of storms, fallen trees, or transformer failures, follow these life-saving procedures.

Emergency Hotline
1912

Toll-free 24x7 Control Room for live wire accidents & transformer fires.

Call 1912 Now
Downed Power Lines

Always assume downed lines are energized. Stay at least 35 feet (10 meters) away. Do not touch wet ground, puddles, or metallic fences touching the line.

Rule: Shuffle feet without lifting if tingling is felt.
Electrical Fires

NEVER throw water on an electrical fire. Water conducts electricity and causes fatal shock. Use Class C (CO2 or Dry Chemical Powder) extinguishers only.

Action: Disconnect main breaker if safe to approach.
Generator Safety

Never run portable fuel generators indoors or in garages due to lethal Carbon Monoxide. Never "backfeed" into home wall sockets—it can electrocute utility linemen working on the line.

Must have: Certified transfer switch.
Surge Protection

When power cuts occur, immediately switch off air conditioners, computers, microwaves, and motors. When power is restored, sudden voltage spikes can damage sensitive electronics.

Tip: Wait 5 minutes after restoration before restarting ACs.
